# Homepage surface brief

## Scope

Homepage only (`/`). Persuade. Rebuild its visual composition within Radiance’s established dark-violet and editorial-serif identity. Keep the live `/chat` route, current feature truth, privacy disclosure, FAQ, interactive demonstration, and model-selection facts.

## Audience and action

People who want an AI to retain context they choose. Help them understand explicit saved notes and response preferences, then lead them to start a chat with Radiance.

## Proof and content

Lead with an honest example of a later question and reply annotated by a saved note and a response preference. Keep demonstration data labeled and disconnected from the visitor’s profile. Keep the full Personal Intelligence flow, current storage limitations and opt-in sharing, current/future distinction, FAQ, and privacy route link. Do not invent social proof or capabilities.

## Constraints

Preserve the Radiance name and existing mark, dark violet identity, editorial serif display voice, Next App Router, current component boundaries and accessible native controls. All new motion must respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep `/chat` and `/privacy` functional.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A conversation is easier to continue when the context a person chose sits in its margin. Refuse the standard AI landing arrangement of a generic promise over a feature-card grid.

**OWN-WORLD:** Build an annotated reader’s folio from Radiance’s existing near-black violet ground, violet ink, warm-white text, editorial serif headlines, and precise marginal rules. The world belongs in the structure of the page, not as a book illustration.

**STORY:** Visitors see a real kind of question beside one saved note and one chosen answer preference; they learn sharing is opt-in and start the actual chat.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Keep the compact Radiance masthead at the top. Set a large two-line serif promise in a broad reading column. Put a short accurate explanation and the primary Chat with Radiance action directly below it. Under the title, compose one continuous illustrative conversation in the main column and the related saved note/preference in a narrow right margin, tied by fine cross-reference rules. At 390px, stack the margin below the conversation while keeping the CTA and context proof in the opening scroll.

**FORM:** The Annotated Edition; ranked form 1 of 7 (annotated editions, commonplace folios, project notebooks, correspondence ledgers, library catalogues, editors’ working proofs, research atlases); seed:86ba4140a25e7e04.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction and motion

The user-selected note and preference are the annotations; on first load, one fine reference line draws from the margin to the reply once. Do not animate every section. Keep the line static under reduced motion and preserve all copy and context.

## Exit condition

Verify the first screen, full-page reading order, keyboard navigation, menu, FAQ, preview steps, privacy link, and real chat CTAs at desktop and mobile widths. Finish with the Impeccable review and documentation step.
