# Landing-page design research

Research reviewed on 2026-11-09. This is a principles log, not an implementation brief to reproduce any reference.

## References inspected

- Stripe homepage, https://stripe.com/ — its above-fold positioning is concise and product-oriented. We selected a clear value proposition paired with substantial application UI, not a replica of its artwork or layout.
- Linear homepage, https://linear.app/ — navigation and text hierarchy stay calm while the product remains the focus. We selected restrained type hierarchy, generous spacing, and deliberate attention control.
- Framer homepage, https://www.framer.com/ — expressive product-led presentation and a clear path from headline to action. We selected a vivid but bounded atmospheric treatment.
- Raycast homepage, https://www.raycast.com/ — individual capabilities are communicated through product demonstrations. We selected concrete UI illustrations instead of long feature explanations.
- Perplexity homepage, https://www.perplexity.ai/ — the interface is the starting point, foregrounding the core interaction. We selected visible conversation context and a strong direct-to-chat action.
- 21st.dev homepage, https://21st.dev/ — a broad index of React UI components, hero experiments, backgrounds, and AI chat components. Browsed for discovery only. No component source was incorporated; the existing project design boundaries and need for a coherent, product-specific interface favored custom components.
- Godly design gallery, https://godly.design/ — curated interface and site examples. We selected variety of composition and section-level contrast, not any particular site's visual treatment.
- Awwwards, https://www.awwwards.com/ — inspected its current awards and trend categories, including animation, scrolling, interactions, and WebGL. We selected purposeful restrained motion, while avoiding effects that impede accessibility or product comprehension.
- Vercel homepage, https://vercel.com/ — its technical navigation groups a broad platform into legible categories. We selected clear information architecture without copying its monochrome visual language.
- Anthropic homepage, https://www.anthropic.com/ — a direct institutional/product statement sits above dated product announcements. We selected accuracy and credibility in the product narrative.
- Land-book gallery, https://land-book.com/ — its gallery supports filtering by color and style. We selected section-level contrast and color as a means to evaluate composition, not as a source for a particular layout.
- Lapa Ninja gallery, https://www.lapa.ninja/ — its catalog spans AI, SaaS, gradients, bento grids, and typography. We selected product specificity over generic trend decoration.

## Principles selected for Radiance

1. Show the working product model early. The app preview is a substantial visual anchor, not a narrow decorative chat.
2. Keep the core story literal and accurate: explicit notes and preferences, bounded lexical retrieval, and user-controlled provider sharing.
3. Shift among light editorial surfaces and selected dark/colored surfaces to create rhythm. Blue/cyan mark intelligence and flow, violet/rose personal context, emerald privacy, and warm sunset CTA.
4. Use color as semantic guidance rather than a repeated rainbow gradient. Reserve energetic blends for hero/future/CTA atmosphere.
5. Use modern sans-serif hierarchy, legible application typography, and purposeful motion that degrades under reduced-motion preferences.
6. Keep direct paths into the real `/chat` application and real existing legal/navigation routes.

## Visual direction

**Radiance Context Atlas** — a bright, confident product canvas with blue-to-cyan signal lines and small moments of coral, violet, and emerald. The recognizable anchor is a large, framed conversation view connected visually to a compact personal-context panel. The page alternates warm white, pale blue, and selective deep/colored surfaces rather than using a site-wide dark canvas.
