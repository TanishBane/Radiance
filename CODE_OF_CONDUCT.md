# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the **Radiance** community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- **Demonstrating empathy and kindness:** Treating fellow contributors, reviewers, and users with respect and patience.
- **Respecting differing perspectives:** Engaging constructively with diverse technical viewpoints, design critiques, and operational experiences.
- **Giving and gracefully accepting feedback:** Offering technical critiques that are actionable and respectful, while receiving feedback with an open mind.
- **Accepting responsibility:** Promptly acknowledging mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and focusing on positive corrective actions.
- **Prioritizing community well-being:** Focusing on technical rigor, accessibility, user safety, and the long-term health of the open-source ecosystem.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting, derogatory, or personal attacks directed at individuals or groups.
- Public or private harassment in any form.
- Publishing private identifying information (doxxing), such as physical addresses, private email addresses, or phone numbers, without explicit consent.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, issues, pull requests, or community channels.
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above behaviors.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders and repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers retain the right and responsibility to edit, redact, or delete comments, commits, issues, pull requests, code changes, and other contributions that do not adhere to this Code of Conduct. When appropriate, maintainers will communicate the reasoning behind moderation decisions.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all official Radiance project spaces, including:

- The GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit messages.
- Official issue tracker templates and code reviews.
- Any public spaces where an individual is officially representing Radiance or its maintainers (e.g., using an official project handle, presenting project architecture at conferences, or acting as an appointed representative).

---

## Reporting Violations

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported directly to the repository maintainer (**Tanish Bane**) through GitHub:

1. **GitHub Security Advisory / Private Contact:** Open a private report through the [Radiance Security Portal](https://github.com/TanishBane/Radiance/security/advisories/new) or contact the project maintainer via their verified GitHub profile: [@TanishBane](https://github.com/TanishBane).
2. **Report Details:** To help maintainers evaluate the situation effectively, please include:
   - A description of the incident and where it occurred (links to issues, PRs, or comments).
   - The individuals involved.
   - Any relevant context or screenshots.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and investigated fairly. The maintainers are committed to respecting the privacy and confidentiality of anyone reporting an incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining consequences for actions deemed in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction
- **Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.
- **Consequence:** A private, written reprimand providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning
- **Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions that disrupt community collaboration.
- **Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time.

### 3. Temporary Ban
- **Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained disruptive behavior or harassment.
- **Consequence:** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved is permitted during this period.

### 4. Permanent Ban
- **Community Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained harassment of an individual, aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals, or doxxing.
- **Consequence:** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project community.
